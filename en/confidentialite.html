In one sentence. Everything you type into Le compteur — games, names, your numbers — stays on your phone. The only thing that leaves it is an anonymous count of games, which you can switch off.

What stays on your phone

This data is never sent, to us or to anyone. It lives in your browser's local storage and nobody but you has access to it.

To erase everything at once: uninstall the app, or clear the site's data in your browser settings. We hold no copy, so we can neither return it to you nor delete it for you — it never reached us.

The names you type belong to other people

When you write "Léa" to compose a team, you are writing someone else's name. It stays on your phone, like a note in a notebook, and we never see it. We simply ask you to treat it like a notebook: write only what the person would accept to see written, and erase it when it is no longer needed.

What leaves the phone

The game counter

When a game ends, the app sends a message that says this, and nothing else:

{"p_evenement":"partie_finie","p_format":2}

That is: a game was finished, in doubles.

No score. No name. No device identifier, no session number, nothing that could recognise you from one game to the next or link two games together. The date is added by the server, not by your phone.

Why. To know whether the tool is really used, before adding anything to it. These are daily aggregate counters, consulted by the publisher alone.

How to switch it off. Menu → The numbers → Don't count my games. The app works exactly the same.

Legal basis. Legitimate interest (article 6.1.f of the GDPR): measuring the use of a tool without identifying anyone. Since no personal data is recorded, there is nothing to access, correct or delete in these counters — but objecting is immediate and unconditional, with the switch above.

What the host sees

As with any site you open, the server answering you sees your IP address and your browser type, for the duration of the request. We keep none of it and do nothing with it. These technical traces belong to our providers, listed below.

The administration account

A page reserved for the publisher (petanque.win/#admin) displays the counters. Signing in requires an e-mail address and a password. These credentials concern only the publisher and any collaborators — never the players. No account is offered to users of the app, and sign-up is disabled.

What we never do

  • No advertising, of any kind.
  • No advertising tracker, no third-party analytics, no social-network button, no font loaded from another site.
  • No sale, no sharing, no transfer of anything to anyone.
  • No phone permission: no location, no contacts, no microphone, no camera, no address book.
  • No cookies. The app uses the browser's local storage only to make what you ask of it work — keeping the game you are counting. That is a use strictly necessary to the requested service, which requires no consent.

Children

The app asks for no account, no address and no age: a child can use it to count a game the way they would use a calculator, without anything about them being recorded anywhere. We do not knowingly collect any data from a minor.

If you write a child's name to compose a team, it stays on your phone and is your responsibility, like a note written in a notebook.

Your rights

The GDPR gives you rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability. Here they mostly exercise themselves: the data about you does not leave your phone; you access and erase it directly, without asking us.

For any question: paul@hempin.org or contact@petanque.win. You may also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL.

Changes

This page carries its update date. If the way things work changes — for example the day player accounts appear — the page will be changed before the change, not after, and the app will say so.
