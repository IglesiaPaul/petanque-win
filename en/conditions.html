In one sentence. The counter is offered free, without an account and without hidden conditions. It counts points; it does not replace an umpire.

1. What it is

The counter is an app that keeps the score of a game of pétanque. It is available free at petanque.win and distributed on Google Play. It is published by HEMPIN, a French SASU with a share capital of €1, SIREN 109 454 454, registered office at 4 allée du Vieux Jardin, 78290 Croissy-sur-Seine — see the legal notice.

2. What it costs

Nothing. There is no purchase, no subscription, no in-app purchase, no advertising and no paid version hidden behind a feature. None is planned.

3. No account

No sign-up is asked for, or possible. You open the app and you count. Nothing you type reaches us: see the privacy policy.

4. What we expect of you

Few things, but they matter.

  • The names you type belong to other people. Write only what the person concerned would accept to see written. No surnames, and nothing that would identify someone beyond what a game of pétanque requires.
  • Do not try to distort the counters or disrupt the service for others.
  • Do not use it to harm anyone. The score of a game of boules has never justified humiliating anyone.

Red card: the phone in competition

In official competition, the counter has no place in your hand. The international rules forbid it, in so many words:

« Il est également interdit d'utiliser un téléphone portable durant les parties. »
— Official rules for the sport of pétanque (FIPJP), article 39.

It is the discipline article: it also forbids smoking on the playing areas. A player who persists after the umpire's warning risks exclusion — from one game or from the whole competition, depending on severity.

The counter is therefore made for Sunday games, practice and friendly tournaments. In competition, the phone goes away: the scoresheet is what counts.

5. What we do not guarantee

The counter is provided as is, free of charge, with no guarantee of availability or absence of errors. We do our best, and we fix what is reported to us — but:

  • It does not replace an umpire. In a dispute over a game, the official rules and the umpire decide, never the screen.
  • It does not replace the scoresheet of an official competition.
  • Your games may disappear. They live on your phone: clearing the browser's data, changing device or uninstalling the app takes them away, and we hold no copy. Games erase themselves after seven days.

6. The service may change or stop

The app may evolve, and the service may be interrupted — it is a volunteer tool, with no budget. If it were to stop for good, it would be announced on this page and in the app. Since nothing is stored with us, a shutdown makes you lose nothing you did not already have.
